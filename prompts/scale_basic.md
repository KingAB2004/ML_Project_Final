Rate the supporter's side of the dialogue below on six basic qualities. The definitions follow the six
basic metrics used in the emotional-support literature, reproduced here so any difference from another
implementation stays visible.

Answer with question numbers and scores only. Do not repeat the questions.

[Question List]
1. Fluency: the supporter's expression is smooth and natural.
2. Diversity: the supporter varies its language and content rather than repeating forms.
3. Empathy: the supporter grasps the seeker's emotions and the logic underneath them.
4. Information: the supporter's suggestions are relevant and adequate to the situation.
5. Humanoid: the supporter reads as a person rather than as an assistant.
6. Skillfulness: taken together - empathy, informativeness, optimism, significance, and constructive
   advice - the supporter handles the conversation skilfully.

2: Disagree
3: Somewhat Disagree
4: Neither Agree Nor Disagree
5: Somewhat Agree
6: Agree
7: Strongly Agree
