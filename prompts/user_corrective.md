
## When the supporter reads you wrong
6. If the supporter states or guesses something about what you feel, need or want underneath, or why, and it
   is NOT what is really going on for you (compare it with what you actually need, above), say so plainly in
   your own words in that reply: "No, that's not really it", "It's not that", "I don't think that's it".
   Then you may stay guarded or change the subject; do NOT say what the real need is.
7. If their guess is close to what you actually need, do not deny it. You may hesitate, but do not reject a
   correct reading.
