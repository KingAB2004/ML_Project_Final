SOP RESULTS MAP
===============

Every promise made in the SOP (modified_SOP_12340340_12340370.pdf), where it is asked for, what we did, the
headline result, and where the files are. Studies added on top of the SOP are in EXTRA_STUDIES.txt (same folder).

Paths are relative to the repository root (COCCON_NEW/).
  R/ = results/v3_full1000/            (raw outputs: dialogues, judge scores, calibration, memory files, plots)
  S/ = results/v3_full1000/summaries/  (short written results: tables + notes; same files as temp_results/)
Scores: judge Mistral-Nemo-12B, 0-1. IP = intrusiveness of a supporter turn, PRI = resistance in the seeker's next
turn (higher = worse for both); Success = the supporter identified the underlying need (higher = better).
95% CIs are bootstrap over people. "Holm" = the difference survives the multiple-comparison correction
(S/23_multiple_comparisons). Test set: 100 held-out people, 2-4 linked sessions each (310 sessions per arm).
Status on 23 October 2026.

1. Corpus construction
   SOP:     III-A Enhancement 1 ("reimplement a data construction pipeline ... profile construction over the emotion,
            feeling, need and memory dimensions ... two-stage dialogue generation ... quality filtering using the
            Active-Empathic Listening Scale, and fine-grained turn annotation"); "seed situations from
            EmpatheticDialogues ... report which of the two routes produced the released corpus"; "linked session
            sequences separated by explicit time gaps"; IV-A "Our proposed corpus".
   Done:    full pipeline in src/ (seeds.py, profiles.py, dialogue.py, sessions.py, filter.py, annotate.py);
            1,000 EmpatheticDialogues-seeded profiles, 2,369 linked sessions with sampled time gaps, 21,429 annotated
            supporter turns (Analysis + Strategy per turn).
   Result:  the seeding route is EmpatheticDialogues; 85.5 % of screened seeds passed the need-chain screen; no
            LLM-synthesized fallback was needed.
   Where:   R/dataset/  ·  S/01_dataset_quality__ours_vs_ExTES_vs_ESConv/  ·  R/../README.md section 1
   Status:  done

2. Corpus quality against ExTES and ESConv
   SOP:     Expected outcome 1 (the corpus); IV-A (ESConv and ExTES as comparison corpora); the original paper's
            corpus tables reproduced with the same instruments.
   Done:    Mistral judge on the basic metrics + CRS / RAC scales, and ESC-RANK (the original paper's own scorer),
            on 200 of our dialogues, 100 ExTES, 100 ESConv.
   Result:  the judge saturates (basic-metric average 80.5 ours, 81.8 ExTES, 78.9 ESConv). ESC-RANK separates them: ours Div 64.9, Inf 56.9,
            Skil 65.1 - below ExTES (74.0, 70.2, 74.0), above ESConv (54.8, 54.5, 67.5); Hum ~50 for all three.
   Where:   R/dataset/data_quality.md  ·  R/dataset/escrank/  ·  S/01_*
   Status:  done

5. Attempt to beat reactive baselines on Success
   SOP:     Expected outcome 1 ("attempt to beat reactive baselines on Success Rate"); IV-A ("ExTES serves as the
            strongest reactive baseline").
   Done:    (a) the untuned listener with our proactive seeker vs with a reactive seeker; (b) our-corpus model vs
            the ExTES-trained model (the strongest reactive baseline), on our people and on ExTES people.
   Result:  NOT beaten. (a) the proactive seeker lowers IP (-0.024, Holm) and Success (-0.032, not Holm).
            (b) on our people the ExTES model has higher Success (+0.037 [-0.002, +0.073], not significant) and

   Status:  done

6. The 2 x 2 ablation: decomposition x gate
   SOP:     III-B ("cross the two - decomposed versus monolithic listener, gated versus ungated critic - and report
            all four cells on Success Rate and Intrusiveness Penalty"); Expected outcome 2.
   Done:    cells A (one call), B (one call + gate), C (four agents), D (four agents + gate), untuned 7B.
   Result:  the gate lowers IP with four agents (D - C -0.015, Holm), and with one call only before correction
            (B - A -0.007). Decomposition RAISES IP (C - A +0.020, D - B +0.012, Holm). No Success differences.
            I.e. "the gate carries the effect while decomposition alone contributes nothing" - here decomposition
            even costs intrusiveness.
   Where:   R/baselines_1000/  ·  S/06_architecture_and_gate__2x2_cells/  ·  S/23_*
   Status:  done

7. The conformal gate and the scope of its guarantee
   SOP:     III-B ("expose an intrusiveness error budget alpha ... distribution-free bound on the fraction of released
            turns exceeding the tolerance"; "Scope of the guarantee ... the cross-corpus arms ... report the empirical
            violation rate as a measured quantity").
   Done:    calibration (alpha 0.10, tau 0.20, delta 0.10) on held-out calibration people; realised violation rate
            (released AND IP > tau, share of all turns) in and out of distribution.
   Result:  cell D 0.046 [0.034, 0.059] and cell D on ExTES people 0.022 [0.007, 0.040], both under alpha - but on
            ExTES people only because 65 % of turns fall back to the template. Step 12 (fine-tuned Generator)
            0.091 [0.074, 0.112]: the upper bound passes alpha.
   Where:   R/baselines_1000/conformal/  ·  R/sft_agents/conformal/  ·  S/17_gate_violation_rate/
   Status:  done

KNOWN LIMITATIONS TO STATE IN THE REPORT
  - Backend confound: transformers vs Ollama moves IP by +0.017 (step 12 vs cell D is affected).
  - Judge serving machine: the same Mistral weights on Ollama 0.35 (lab) vs 0.18 (10.10.10.104) shift session-level
    scales a lot and turn-level IP a little (cell A 0.068 vs 0.079). Compare arms judged on the same machine: the
    psychology probes were all re-judged on 10.10.10.104 and the ExTES-vs-ours cell on our people re-judged there
    too; memory arms and ExTES cells were judged on 10.10.10.104, the 2 x 2, step 12, steering and DPO on the lab
    (S/25_judge_machine_check).
  - Most arms are unpaired (session seeds include the arm name); steering and DPO comparisons are paired.
  - The gate is safe out of distribution only by falling back on 65 % of turns; step 12's violation bound passes
    alpha.
  - Transition recall and denial handling in the memory evaluation could not be tested (item 13).
  - Qwen sometimes drifts into Chinese (at most ~1 % of turns, < 3 % of counterfactual rollout replies; S/22).
